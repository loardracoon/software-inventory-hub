HOW CHANGES REACH CLIENTS
Slugs never change. Renaming a subcategory republishes its entries under the same slug, so client filters and saved searches keep working. Changing a default attention level republishes every entry that inherits it. Entries with their own override keep it. Jev receives the options as “Level 1 / Level 2: description”. If it cannot pick a subcategory, it picks a level 1 first and the entry goes to review. Automatic mode only publishes when both the current and the proposed subcategory are Info and the probability is 0.95 or more.

Categories, subcategories, attention defaults and examples are the proposed taxonomy. Entry counts are sample data.
